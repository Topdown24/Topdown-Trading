iShares ETFs
iShares is a global leader in exchange-traded funds (ETFs) with a lineup of over 1,600 funds globally and approximately $4.6 trillion in assets under management. As part of BlackRock, iShares ETFs offer investors access to diversified, low-cost investment opportunities across global markets. The iShares brand traces its roots to 1996 when the first 17 ETFs launched, originally known as World Equity Benchmark Shares (WEBS). In 2000, Barclays rebranded the funds as iShares and significantly expanded the product line. BlackRock acquired the iShares business from Barclays in 2009, establishing it as the world's largest ETF provider. iShares pioneered several ETF innovations, including the first international country ETFs (1996), the first U.S. bond ETFs (2002), and the first sustainable fixed income ETFs (2017). Today, iShares serves over 100 million investors worldwide through a comprehensive range of index and actively managed ETFs covering equity, fixed income, commodity, and alternative asset classes. The sponsor of iShares ETFs is iShares Delaware Trust Sponsor LLC, an affiliate of BlackRock, Inc. BlackRock Investments, LLC serves as distributor.
