Van Eck ETFs
VanEck was founded by John C. van Eck in 1955 during the post-World War II recovery, launching one of the first U.S. mutual funds focused on international markets. This pioneering approach established VanEck as a firm willing to look beyond conventional ideas and act with conviction to provide investors meaningful portfolio exposures. The firm has been privately owned and operated by the van Eck family since its founding. In 1968, VanEck launched the first U.S. mutual fund dedicated to gold equities, recognizing the investment implications of shifting monetary and macroeconomic conditions shortly before gold was decoupled from the dollar. The firm began managing emerging markets assets in 1993 and founded its ETF business in 2006 with the launch of Market Vectors ETFs, including the first gold ETF based on miner shares. In 2011, VanEck launched its index company, Market Vectors Index Solutions. As of December 31, 2025, VanEck manages approximately $181.4 billion in assets across exchange-traded funds, mutual funds, institutional funds, separately managed accounts, model portfolios, and UCITS funds. The firm's capabilities range from core investment opportunities to specialized exposures designed to enhance portfolio diversification. VanEck offers both active and passive investment strategies supported by intelligently designed investment processes. Jan van Eck joined the firm in 1991 and has served as President and Chief Executive Officer since 2010. He serves as President and CEO of Van Eck Associates Corporation and Van Eck Securities Corporation, and as Trustee, President and Chief Executive Officer of VanEck ETF Trust, VanEck Funds and VanEck VIP Trust. VanEck is headquartered in New York with offices and local product offerings in Europe, Australia, Asia and Latin America. Van Eck Securities Corporation serves as distributor for VanEck ETFs.
