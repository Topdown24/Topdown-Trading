ProShares ETFs
ProShares has been at the forefront of the ETF revolution since 2006, when it launched the first leveraged and inverse exchange-traded funds in the United States. The firm manages one of the industry's largest ETF lineups and, together with its mutual fund affiliate ProFunds, oversees more than $100 billion in assets as of October 2025. ProShares is structured as a family of exchange-traded funds organized under multiple Delaware statutory trusts, with ProShare Advisors LLC serving as investment advisor. The company pioneered several ETF innovations, including the introduction of the first short and leveraged ETFs on June 21, 2006, making sophisticated investment strategies accessible through simple-to-use exchange-traded fund vehicles. In December 2006, ProShares launched the first ETF to convert from closed-end fund structure. In October 2021, ProShares introduced the first U.S. bitcoin-linked ETF. The firm is a leader in multiple investment strategies including dividend growth, high income, interest rate hedged bond, cryptocurrency-linked, and geared (leveraged and inverse) ETF investing. ProShares is the world's largest provider of geared funds, managing approximately 99% of the nation's short and leveraged ETF assets. The company's investment approach enables investors to execute sophisticated strategies including hedging portfolios, taking advantage of market trends, and amplifying investment exposure through a single trade. ProShares operates as part of ProFunds Group, which was founded in 1997. ProFunds pioneered leveraged and inverse mutual fund strategies, providing investors with access to sophisticated investment techniques since 1997. ProShares ETFs are distributed by SEI Investments Distribution Co., which is not affiliated with the funds' advisor or sponsor. Information gathered from company website.
