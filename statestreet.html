State Street ETFs
State Street Investment Management, headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, was founded in 1978 as the asset management division of State Street Corporation. The firm is the fourth-largest asset manager in the world, managing approximately $4.67 trillion in assets as of March 31, 2025. State Street Investment Management serves a spectrum of clients across more than 60 countries, from institutional and individual investors to plan sponsors and financial advisors. The firm rebranded from State Street Global Advisors to State Street Investment Management in 2025. As pioneers in indexing and ETF investing, State Street Investment Management has a decades-long track record of innovation in investment solutions. The firm launched the first U.S.-listed exchange-traded fund on January 22, 1993, in partnership with the American Stock Exchange. The firm also pioneered international ETF markets, launching the first ETFs in Hong Kong (November 11, 1999), Australia (August 24, 2001), and Singapore (April 11, 2002). SPDR (pronounced "spider") is a family of exchange-traded funds managed by State Street Investment Management. SPDR is an acronym for Standard & Poor's Depositary Receipts, the name of the first fund in the family, the SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY). In December 1998, State Street introduced the Select Sector SPDR ETFs, creating the industry's first suite of sector-based ETFs. As of September 30, 2025, the Select Sector SPDR suite manages over $332 billion in net assets across all eleven S&P 500 sectors. The SPDR family encompasses a comprehensive range of index and actively managed ETFs covering domestic and international equities, fixed income, commodities, and alternatives. As of 2025, State Street manages approximately $1.8 trillion in ETF assets across 175 U.S. products. State Street Global Advisors Funds Distributors, LLC serves as distributor for SPDR ETFs.
