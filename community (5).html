Community Portfolios
  • Once you've registered your Personal Portfolio and chosen your Moniker, your portfolio's average IR score is measured daily against every other active member of the community — giving you a live read on where you stand relative to your peers.
  • Curious why another portfolio is outranking yours? Roll your cursor over any Moniker symbol in the list to the left to see the individual issues driving that portfolio's score, ranked by whichever IR period you're currently viewing.
  • The community's top-performing portfolios each quarter earn a recognition badge — a quiet acknowledgment that the reversion-to-mean process is working and that patience is being rewarded.
  • Only active portfolios are displayed here. Any portfolio that has gone 30 days without an adjustment is automatically removed from the community rankings to keep the comparison meaningful and current.
