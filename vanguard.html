Vanguard ETFs
The Vanguard Group, Inc. was founded in 1975 by John C. Bogle with a revolutionary concept: to structure a mutual fund company owned by its fund shareholders rather than outside owners seeking profits. This unique investor-owned structure enables Vanguard to redirect net profits from economies of scale to fund shareholders in the form of lower costs, operating solely in investors' interests. The company is headquartered in Valley Forge, Pennsylvania. In 1976, Vanguard launched the First Index Investment Trust (now the Vanguard 500 Index Fund), creating the first index mutual fund for individual investors. This innovation fundamentally transformed the investment industry by making broad market exposure accessible at low cost. Since pioneering indexing, Vanguard has introduced numerous industry firsts including international indexing, small-cap indexing, bond indexing, and emerging markets indexing. Vanguard is one of the world's leading investment management companies, offering investments, advice, and retirement services to tens of millions of individual investors globally through direct accounts, workplace retirement plans, and financial intermediaries. As of November 2025, Vanguard manages more than 65 index mutual funds and nearly 70 index ETFs tracking benchmarks across bond and stock markets, both U.S. and international, as well as sector-specific market areas. The firm's investor-owned structure remains unique in the investment industry. Vanguard fund shareholders own the funds, which in turn own Vanguard. This alignment of interests has enabled the company to steadily lower its asset-weighted average expense ratio from 0.68% in 1975 to 0.09% by 2020. In February 2025, Vanguard announced its largest-ever expense ratio reduction, affecting 168 mutual fund and ETF share classes across 87 funds. Vanguard Marketing Corporation serves as distributor for Vanguard mutual funds and ETFs. Information gathered from company website.
