First Trust ETFs
First Trust Portfolios L.P. and First Trust Advisors L.P. were founded in 1991 and are headquartered in Wheaton, Illinois. The firms operate as sister companies with a shared mission to offer investors trusted investment products and advisory services. First Trust Advisors L.P. was formed as an Illinois limited partnership with The Charger Corporation serving as general partner. As of December 2025, First Trust Advisors L.P. manages approximately $189.9 billion in assets and serves over 5,000 clients. The firm provides investment management services across multiple product structures including unit investment trusts, exchange-traded funds, mutual funds, closed-end funds, and separately managed accounts for institutional and retail investors. First Trust's investment capabilities cover equity, fixed income, and alternative asset classes. First Trust pioneered several innovations in the ETF industry. In December 2006, the firm launched the First Trust Value Line Dividend Index Fund, which became the first closed-end fund to convert to an exchange-traded fund structure. First Trust developed and trademarked the AlphaDEX methodology, a rules-based selection process that enhances traditional indexing concepts. The AlphaDEX trademark has been licensed to index providers for use in creating specialized market indexes. The firm's approach to investing emphasizes five core principles: know what you own, invest for the long-term, employ discipline, rebalance, and control taxes. First Trust primarily serves the financial advisor market, working closely with investment professionals who use First Trust products to help their clients define goals and develop long-term investment strategies. First Trust Portfolios L.P. serves as principal underwriter for First Trust exchange-traded funds. Information gathered from company website.
