GlobalX ETFs
Global X was founded in 2008 with the mission of listening to and empowering clients to invest wisely in unexplored and intelligent solutions. The firm's product lineup includes more than 75 ETF strategies designed to provide investors with access to a diverse range of investment opportunities. As of August 2025, Global X manages over $50 billion in assets across ETFs listed worldwide. Global X Management Company, LLC serves as investment advisor to the Global X Funds and is an indirect, wholly-owned subsidiary of Mirae Asset Global Investments Co., Ltd. For select actively managed strategies, the firm utilizes sub-advisory relationships with Mirae Asset Global Investments (USA) LLC and Mirae Asset Global Investments (Hong Kong) Limited, leveraging specialized regional expertise and research capabilities across global markets. The firm is distinguished for its thematic growth, income, and international access ETFs, while also offering core, commodity, and alpha funds to suit a wide range of investment objectives. Global X's investment philosophy emphasizes identifying trends across technology, income strategies, and emerging economies before they become mainstream. The firm employs a team of more than 200 financial professionals dedicated to developing intelligently designed investment strategies. Global X is headquartered at 605 3rd Avenue, 43rd Floor, New York, NY. The funds are distributed by SEI Investments Distribution Co., which is not affiliated with Global X Management Company, LLC or Mirae Asset Global Investments.
