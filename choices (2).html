Personal Portfolio Choices
Complete these five steps to personalize your Personal Portfolio
  • Step 1 — Portfolio Moniker — Name your Personal Portfolio by making three choices from our special pull-down menus
  • Step 2 — Histogram Background — Choose OEX or NDX as the background index for your Personal Portfolio Histogram
  • Step 3 — Research ETFs and Indexes — Make up to six ETF or Index choices to follow for personal sector research
  • Step 4 — Portfolio Issues — Make up to ten Issue or Index choices to add to your Personal Portfolio Tracker
  • Step 5 — Portfolio Sharing — Choose whether or not to share your Portfolio with the Portfolio Community
Step 1. Choose your three-word Portfolio Moniker from our special selection menus. Once you make a selection, the next menu will appear. Your new Portfolio Moniker will debut as the new name of your My Portfolio page.

Enter an Index or ETF symbol to follow as sector research for your Personal Portfolio. Limit of 6. These entries appear in your Personal Histogram and on your My Portfolio Sector section, but are not added to the Personal Portfolio Tracker.
Step 4. Select as many as 10 equities, ETFs or indexes to appear on your Personal Portfolio page for research. Your choices appear immediately as research — you can study the 90/10 chart and IR readings before committing. Nothing enters your Portfolio Tracker until you click Buy on your My Portfolio page. At that point the position opens at that day's closing price and your Buy IR is recorded. Your research choices remain visible after you Buy or Sell, so you can keep watching the IR cycle and decide when to re-enter.

Step 5. Choose whether or not to share your Portfolio with the Portfolio Community. If you choose Share, your portfolio will be identified only by your Portfolio Moniker — which is known only by you. Sharing activates the Portfolio Community button on your My Portfolio page.
