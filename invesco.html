Invesco ETFs
Invesco Ltd. is a global independent investment management firm headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia. The company was established in 1935 and currently operates in more than 20 countries worldwide. Invesco is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol IVZ and manages approximately $2.1 trillion in assets as of September 30, 2025. The firm offers institutional and retail clients an expansive array of active and passive investment styles across traditional and alternative investments. Invesco's investment capabilities span equity, fixed income, separately managed accounts, exchange-traded funds, mutual funds, and alternative investment vehicles. The company serves a diverse global clientele including retail investors, institutional investors, defined benefit and defined contribution plans, insurance companies, foundations, endowments, and sovereign wealth funds. In September 2006, Invesco acquired PowerShares Capital Management LLC, significantly expanding its exchange-traded fund capabilities. PowerShares was recognized as an innovator in the ETF marketplace, receiving SEC approval in 2007 as the first firm to operate actively managed equity and fixed-income ETFs. Following the acquisition, the PowerShares brand was integrated as Invesco PowerShares, combining Invesco's global resources with PowerShares' ETF expertise. In 2007, the company rebranded from AMVESCAP PLC to Invesco Ltd. and redomiciled from the United Kingdom to Bermuda. The firm moved its headquarters from London to Atlanta in November 2007. Invesco Distributors, Inc. serves as the principal underwriter for Invesco exchange-traded funds. Information gathered from company website.
